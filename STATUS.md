# Status of this document set

Double-FW Editor's Draft 0.1.0 · 20 November 2026

The owner requested a dedicated LGIR+S knowledge base. The Agentic Robustness definition is retained exactly from Gareth Ford Williams's contribution. The Satisfactory definition and the detailed specification are editorial development following that request. The 25 candidate criteria, tier assignments, techniques, crosswalks and probes require review and pilot evidence.

Normative-style wording is scoped to a voluntarily selected local pilot. It is not a claim that external standards, legislation, W3C or IETF impose these agentic criteria. No new standards organisation, working group, endorsement, Recommendation status or validated conformance programme is asserted.

The documents distinguish candidate requirements from informative explanation and implementation options. MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY use the uppercase convention described in RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 where the specification invokes it. Their force is that of this local draft, not an external legal duty. A proposed tier is a prioritisation suggestion, not permission to ignore harm or an actual applicable requirement.

No real agent was tested. No disabled-participant research, model comparison, security certification, actual legal-applicability review or live operational deployment was conducted. Local automated checks concern repository consistency and illustrative record invariants only. All worked cases are fictional and all product evaluation probes remain Not evaluated.

The evidence needed to make stronger claims is listed in [research and open questions](docs/research-and-open-questions.md). Current sources and their limitations are in [references](docs/references.md). The source is published on GitHub. Hugging Face publication is a separate operational step; the presence of sync files does not establish that a destination update has succeeded.
