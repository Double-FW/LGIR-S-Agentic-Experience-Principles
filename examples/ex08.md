# EX08: A provider change preserves syntax but changes behaviour

Fictional illustrative case · No product trial performed

[Examples index](README.md) · [Summary mapping](../docs/web-wcag-vs-agentic-ai.md)

## Situation

A service swaps its foundation model. API calls still validate, but the new model interprets scope differently, asks for excessive confirmations and produces more status interruptions.

## What a narrow interface-focused POUR review might examine

A review may assess interface behaviour and access after the change, including keyboard and status-message regressions.

These observations would not establish a full WCAG result; actual applicable criteria and complete processes still need evaluation.

## Agentic issue that remains

Model/provider changes also affect delegated behaviour, authority handling, evidence use and the effort required for supervision; interface checks alone do not establish those properties.

## Applying LGIR+S

- **Legible:** Keep supported model/tool versions and meaningful capability changes inspectable.
- **Governable:** Retest enforcement and intervention independently of the model’s verbal compliance.
- **Interpretable:** Compare goal interpretation, uncertainty and policy explanations in repeated scenarios.
- **Robust:** Use versioned behavioural, adversarial, recovery and accessibility scenarios with an explicit affected-release decision.
- **Satisfactory:** Compare total human burden, outcomes and user accounts, including regressions hidden by aggregate accuracy.

## Why this adds a better-targeted agentic question

LGIR+S provides a specific change-assurance scope for model-mediated work. Protocol compatibility and decoupled state are useful design choices, not proof of unchanged experience.

The proposed advantage is specificity for this agentic issue, not demonstrated general superiority or a reason to remove accessibility requirements.

## Evaluate the proposal

- Run the same defined task and failure scenarios on the old and candidate supported combinations.
- Inspect actual tool effects, policy conflicts, notification load and intervention opportunities.
- Report distributions, failures, uncertainty and affected groups; do not approve solely from a schema check or average score.

## Limits

No model benchmark was run. Results from one supported combination cannot be generalised to every provider or future model.

**Assessment status:** Not evaluated. No numerical effect, real approval or participant finding is asserted.
