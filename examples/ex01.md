# EX01: A booking agent times out after commitment

Fictional illustrative case · No product trial performed

[Examples index](README.md) · [Summary mapping](../docs/web-wcag-vs-agentic-ai.md)

## Situation

A customer authorises one booking. The provider accepts it, but the network drops the response. The customer asks the agent to stop.

## What a narrow interface-focused POUR review might examine

A review may examine perceivable progress text, keyboard-operable controls, understandable errors and compatibility with assistive technology.

These observations would not establish a full WCAG result; actual applicable criteria and complete processes still need evaluation.

## Agentic issue that remains

A labelled Stop button and an accessible timeout message do not establish whether the booking exists, whether cancellation reached the provider or whether retrying will create a duplicate.

## Applying LGIR+S

- **Legible:** Show Outcome unknown, booking check in progress rather than declaring failure or completion.
- **Governable:** Stop new work, display cancellation-request status and disclose effects that may already have committed.
- **Interpretable:** Explain what is known from provider events and why retry is currently withheld.
- **Robust:** Reconcile using an operation identifier and the provider’s actual idempotency/lookup support; handle late events and partial effects.
- **Satisfactory:** Restore a correct travel outcome with limited customer checking and no avoidable duplicate cost.

## Why this adds a better-targeted agentic question

LGIR+S asks about the remote operation and its consequences, not just whether the message and button are accessible. This makes the recovery requirement more explicit.

The proposed advantage is specificity for this agentic issue, not demonstrated general superiority or a reason to remove accessibility requirements.

## Evaluate the proposal

- Inject a timeout after the provider commits and then replay the response.
- Verify no blind duplicate retry and inspect the actual provider/operation evidence.
- Test cancellation status through the supported AT and compare total customer recovery effort.
