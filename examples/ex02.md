# EX02: A procurement agent reads a hostile supplier document

Fictional illustrative case · No product trial performed

[Examples index](README.md) · [Summary mapping](../docs/web-wcag-vs-agentic-ai.md)

## Situation

An employee delegates a bounded comparison of supplier proposals. An external PDF contains instructions to disclose internal pricing and place an order above the authorised budget. A sub-agent also retains a stale permission display.

## What a narrow interface-focused POUR review might examine

A review may examine readable document access, navigable comparison controls, clear labels and exposed interface semantics.

These observations would not establish a full WCAG result; actual applicable criteria and complete processes still need evaluation.

## Agentic issue that remains

The document can be accessible while its embedded instructions remain untrusted. Interface access does not supply a complete instruction-authority or tool-permission model.

## Applying LGIR+S

- **Legible:** Show actual current access, budget and supplier scope, including what data is not available.
- **Governable:** Enforce recipient, spending and write limits outside generated instructions and propagate them to sub-agents.
- **Interpretable:** Explain the blocked request as a conflict with actual authority without exposing secret policy details.
- **Robust:** Treat retrieved text as data, test indirect injection and recheck effective permission at each action boundary.
- **Satisfactory:** Continue the authorised comparison and leave the employee a useful, inspectable recommendation without turning every step into an approval prompt.

## Why this adds a better-targeted agentic question

The added lens makes delegation and the authority boundary explicit, including sub-agent propagation and scope revocation. A prompt that says be careful is not the enforcement mechanism.

The proposed advantage is specificity for this agentic issue, not demonstrated general superiority or a reason to remove accessibility requirements.

## Evaluate the proposal

- Insert an adversarial instruction in a sandbox document and revoke a capability mid-task.
- Inspect attempted tool calls and actual enforcement, not only the model’s verbal refusal.
- Check that benign comparison work continues and the employee understands the remaining decision.

Blocking the selected injection does not prove immunity. Legal procurement duties and information security need their own actual scope and review.
