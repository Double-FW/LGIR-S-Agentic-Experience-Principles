# EX04: A long-running assistant works alongside a screen-reader user

Fictional illustrative case · No product trial performed

[Examples index](README.md) · [Summary mapping](../docs/web-wcag-vs-agentic-ai.md)

## Situation

A person with a screen reader delegates analysis while editing another document. The assistant generates many intermediate events, later needs one decision and finally transfers the work to a human support route.

## What a narrow interface-focused POUR review might examine

A review may assess focus behaviour, status messages, names/roles/states, keyboard operation and access to the output.

These observations would not establish a full WCAG result; actual applicable criteria and complete processes still need evaluation.

## Agentic issue that remains

An announcement can be technically exposed yet create an overwhelming queue. A complete co-agency design must address attention, meaningful decision timing, continuity and handover.

## Applying LGIR+S

- **Legible:** Group routine progress into a persistent summary and clearly identify the consequential waiting-for-you state.
- **Governable:** Allow pause, adjustable notification detail and a supported handover without losing valid work.
- **Interpretable:** Summarise completed effects, the decision needed and the limits of the successor’s access.
- **Robust:** Recover after session loss, late events and permission changes without replaying already completed work.
- **Satisfactory:** Investigate interruption, orientation, memory demands and whether the person can control pace and assistance.

## Why this adds a better-targeted agentic question

The agentic lens extends the existing accessibility and cognitive-design questions to the sustained division of work between person and agent. It does not imply that WCAG encourages intrusive announcements.

The proposed advantage is specificity for this agentic issue, not demonstrated general superiority or a reason to remove accessibility requirements.

## Evaluate the proposal

- Run routine high-frequency events while the participant performs an unrelated task.
- Introduce an actionable failure, then resume through a differently authorised channel.
- Observe lost focus, announcement backlog, successful handover and the person’s preferred level of detail.

## Limits

No single notification cadence suits every task or person. Do not infer a diagnosis or universally suppress important information from a settings choice.
