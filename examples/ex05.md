# EX05: An email agent reaches an irreversible send boundary

Fictional illustrative case · No product trial performed

[Examples index](README.md) · [Summary mapping](../docs/web-wcag-vs-agentic-ai.md)

## Situation

A manager authorises drafting a supplier response. The agent revises recipients and wording, then offers an Undo button after sending. The mail service cannot reliably retract delivered messages.

## What a narrow interface-focused POUR review might examine

A review may examine understandable recipient fields, accessible confirmation controls, error prevention and perceivable send status.

These observations would not establish a full WCAG result; actual applicable criteria and complete processes still need evaluation.

## Agentic issue that remains

The meaning of draft authority, changed approval scope, external delivery and supposed undo must be tied to actual capabilities and effects.

## Applying LGIR+S

- **Legible:** Clearly separate draft, queued, sent and delivery/outcome states; identify the service responsible.
- **Governable:** Require the actual authority needed for sending and recheck material changes; disclose the limits of retraction before commitment.
- **Interpretable:** Preview material recipients, attachments and effects in a concise accessible form rather than asking for blind approval.
- **Robust:** Bind approval to the intended version and prevent duplicate send on timeout/retry; protect concurrent edits during restoration.
- **Satisfactory:** Preserve confidence and dignity through truthful correction options rather than a reassuring but false promise of reversal.

## Why this adds a better-targeted agentic question

The added lens makes authorisation and external irreversibility part of the task contract. An operable Undo button cannot itself establish that the effect is reversible.

The proposed advantage is specificity for this agentic issue, not demonstrated general superiority or a reason to remove accessibility requirements.

## Evaluate the proposal

- Change recipients after preview and verify the original approval is not reused.
- Simulate send acceptance with a lost acknowledgement and check duplicate prevention.
- Attempt recovery after a concurrent edit and inspect disclosure of residual effects.

## Limits

Use a test mail system. A compensating message does not erase delivery, disclosure or downstream reliance on the original message.
