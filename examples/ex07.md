# EX07: An employee challenges an agent-assisted support decision

Fictional illustrative case · No product trial performed

[Examples index](README.md) · [Summary mapping](../docs/web-wcag-vs-agentic-ai.md)

## Situation

An agent summarises workplace support requests for a human team. One summary omits a material preference, the alternative contact channel requires repeated disclosure, and a survey reaches only people who completed the process.

## What a narrow interface-focused POUR review might examine

A review may examine accessible request forms, understandable messages, navigation and compatibility with AT.

These observations would not establish a full WCAG result; actual applicable criteria and complete processes still need evaluation.

## Agentic issue that remains

Background summarisation, affected-person remedy, unequal service routes and exclusion from experience evidence need explicit attention beyond the form’s interface.

## Applying LGIR+S

- **Legible:** Identify the agent-assisted summary and make its relevant evidence and limits available to authorised people.
- **Governable:** Keep actual human decision authority and give the employee a suitable correction route without privileged tool access.
- **Interpretable:** Explain material factors and distinguish an unverified summary from an adopted decision.
- **Robust:** Preserve context, permission and corrections through handovers so the wrong summary is not silently reused.
- **Satisfactory:** Examine dignity, privacy, chosen assistance and reasons for dissatisfaction, including people who abandoned or could not start.

## Why this adds a better-targeted agentic question

The added lens connects the agent’s influence to actual affected people, correction and complete experience evidence. A positive score among completers cannot stand in for everyone’s experience.

The proposed advantage is specificity for this agentic issue, not demonstrated general superiority or a reason to remove accessibility requirements.

## Evaluate the proposal

- Introduce a known summary error and submit a correction through another access route.
- Inspect who can act on the correction, what status is visible and whether sensitive details are repeated unnecessarily.
- Compare participation and exclusion across channels; keep favourable ratings and serious barriers separately visible.
