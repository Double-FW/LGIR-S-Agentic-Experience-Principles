# Start here

## What LGIR+S is for

Use it when an AI system can interpret a goal, use information, propose or take actions, and leave a person or organisation with consequences to understand and manage. Examples include research assistants, booking agents, HR copilots, coding agents and customer-service agents.

The framework asks five connected questions:

1. **Legible:** Can I identify the agent's involvement, relevant information and current state?
2. **Governable:** Can the right person set limits and intervene effectively?
3. **Interpretable:** Can I understand enough about the action and its evidence to make a decision?
4. **Robust:** Can data, execution and oversight remain dependable through relevant failures and changes?
5. **Satisfactory:** Does this actually help me, including the time and work I must spend checking and correcting it?

Satisfactory runs across the other four. High satisfaction does not cancel an access failure, an unauthorised action or a false answer.

## How to read the standards-style structure

- **Specification:** candidate requirement wording for a local review or pilot.
- **Understanding:** intent, applicability, benefits to investigate, examples, evaluation probes and limitations.
- **Implementation:** possible approaches; alternatives can be appropriate if they achieve the scoped outcome.
- **Evaluation:** how to collect evidence and report gaps without overstating the result.

Local tier A means proposed foundation priority; local tier AA means proposed extended assurance priority. They are provisional labels, not WCAG levels or a validated compliance ladder. Every criterion is currently Not evaluated for any real product.

## Relationship to POUR

POUR still organises WCAG accessibility requirements. LGIR+S is more explicit about agentic delegation and consequences. Use both lenses where the service includes a human-facing interface. The case studies illustrate additional issues; they do not prove comparative superiority or show that a full WCAG evaluation would miss every example.
