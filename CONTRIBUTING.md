# Contribute to LGIR+S

Describe the criterion or document affected, the practical ambiguity or failure, the people and contexts involved, the suggested change and public supporting evidence. Include contrary evidence and an example where the new wording could fail. Non-technical feedback is welcome; a proposed plain-English explanation is sufficient.

For substantive changes, preserve the original meaning and stable identifier or document the migration. Update the source record, rationale, applicable examples and evaluation probe together. Do not turn fictional cases or automated integrity tests into real-world validation claims.

External terms retain their authoritative meanings. Avoid copying third-party content without the relevant permission or losing its terms. Do not publish customer, employee or participant-level evidence, security-sensitive operational details or credentials.

Gareth Ford Williams or an explicitly delegated owner can decide the project's wording and scope. Specialist/participant review is needed for the claims it actually evaluates; a Git merge is not independent validation or client policy adoption. Record material decisions in the changelog with scope, evidence and remaining questions.

For generated content, change the canonical data and regenerate. See [Maintenance](MAINTENANCE.md). No external contributor agreement, working group approval or W3C process is asserted by this repository.
