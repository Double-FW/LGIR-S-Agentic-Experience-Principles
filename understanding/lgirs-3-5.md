# Understanding LGIRS-3.5: Challenge, correction and accessible remedy

Informative · Editor’s Draft 0.1.0 · No product result established

[Specification](../SPECIFICATION.md#lgirs-3-5) · [Understanding index](README.md)

## Candidate criterion

People affected by consequential outcomes MUST have an accessible route to question material information, submit corrections and reach the responsible human or service with a traceable response.

**Local priority:** AA (provisional; not WCAG conformance).

## Intent and potential benefit

Interpretability has practical value when a person can act on a discovered problem.

This is a rationale to investigate, not a measured effectiveness claim. Confirm its relevance with the affected people and the actual service.

## Applicability

Affected customers, employees and operators; a person may need remedy without controlling the agent's privileged tools.

## Implementation approach

Link the outcome to case context and a service owner; distinguish proposed corrections from verified changes and avoid requiring repeated sensitive disclosure.

See [technique T15](../implementation/techniques.md#t15). The approach is informative; document and evaluate an alternative if it achieves the scoped outcome.

## Evaluation probe — not yet run

1. Identify the task, roles, authorised scope, product/model/tool versions and expected evidence. Agree any service-specific timing and safety limits before testing.
2. Prepare controlled, synthetic or appropriately authorised test conditions; do not cause real irreversible effects merely to test a failure route.
3. Challenge a factual error through an alternative access route after the original agent session has ended.
4. Observe: The issue reaches the responsible route with appropriate context and a truthful status; the person is not forced to recreate the entire conversation.
5. Capture actual events, accessible presentation and human observations separately. Record missing coverage and contrary evidence. Assign the criterion result using the evaluation method; do not infer effectiveness from this written probe.

## Failure example

The only appeal is Ask the same bot again, and the bot repeatedly declares its own answer correct.

## Evidence and limits

Retain the requirement ID, exact evaluated scope, method, observed outcome, source/version context, reviewer and unresolved gaps. A generated expected result is not an observation. Machine checks, human inspection, specialist evaluation and participant research have different roles. Passing this probe alone does not establish conformance with WCAG or reliability for all contexts.
