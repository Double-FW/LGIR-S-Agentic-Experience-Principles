# Understanding LGIRS-5.5: Experience evidence and continuous improvement

Informative · Editor’s Draft 0.1.0 · No product result established

[Specification](../SPECIFICATION.md#lgirs-5-5) · [Understanding index](README.md)

## Candidate criterion

The service evaluation MUST collect proportionate accessible evidence about whether the experience met people's needs and reasons, connect findings to owned improvement work and preserve contrary evidence and study limitations.

**Local priority:** AA (provisional; not WCAG conformance).

## Intent and potential benefit

Satisfaction is informative when interpreted with outcomes and barriers rather than treated as a universal quality score.

This is a rationale to investigate, not a measured effectiveness claim. Confirm its relevance with the affected people and the actual service.

## Applicability

Research and operational learning, including abandonment and complaints; do not make research participation a condition of service.

## Implementation approach

Ask short purposeful questions, use consent and data minimisation, analyse non-response and route exclusion, and track correction/retest ownership.

See [technique T25](../implementation/techniques.md#t25). The approach is informative; document and evaluate an alternative if it achieves the scoped outcome.

## Evaluation probe — not yet run

1. Identify the task, roles, authorised scope, product/model/tool versions and expected evidence. Agree any service-specific timing and safety limits before testing.
2. Prepare controlled, synthetic or appropriately authorised test conditions; do not cause real irreversible effects merely to test a failure route.
3. Run a pilot in which respondents rate the agent highly but excluded non-completers report a blocking issue through support.
4. Observe: The evaluation preserves both findings, records missing coverage and assigns the barrier for action without calling the high rating proof of conformance.
5. Capture actual events, accessible presentation and human observations separately. Record missing coverage and contrary evidence. Assign the criterion result using the evaluation method; do not infer effectiveness from this written probe.

## Failure example

Only successful users can answer the survey, and the resulting score is described as satisfaction for every customer.

## Evidence and limits

Retain the requirement ID, exact evaluated scope, method, observed outcome, source/version context, reviewer and unresolved gaps. A generated expected result is not an observation. Machine checks, human inspection, specialist evaluation and participant research have different roles. Passing this probe alone does not establish conformance with WCAG or reliability for all contexts.
