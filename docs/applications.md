# Applications by task and role

The framework can be used before implementation, during procurement, in design review, in QA and during operation. Select the actual task and evidence needed; do not assume every application has the same risks or autonomy.

| Application | Important LGIR+S questions | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research and policy drafting | Are sources current for the question, are gaps explicit, and how much verification work remains? | [EX03](../examples/ex03.md); LGIRS-1.4, 3.2, 3.3, 4.1, 5.2 |
| Booking or transaction assistant | What authority exists, did the remote effect commit, and what can actually be stopped or recovered? | [EX01](../examples/ex01.md); LGIRS-1.3, 2.2, 4.3, 4.4 |
| Email or publication agent | Does draft authority include sending, do previews match the committed version, and what is irreversible? | [EX05](../examples/ex05.md); LGIRS-2.3, 2.4, 3.1 |
| Procurement and document processing | Can untrusted source instructions expand tool authority or budget? | [EX02](../examples/ex02.md); LGIRS-1.2, 2.1, 3.4, 4.2 |
| Employee support and HR assistance | Who is affected, can errors be challenged, and are privacy and dignity preserved across routes? | [EX07](../examples/ex07.md); LGIRS-3.5, 5.3–5.5 |
| Accessible personal assistance | Can the person choose pace, modality, support and takeover without excessive interruption? | [EX04](../examples/ex04.md); LGIRS-1.5, 2.5, 5.3 |
| Agent-mediated service planning | Does optimisation preserve the person's material goal and constraints? | [EX06](../examples/ex06.md); LGIRS-3.1, 5.1 |
| Model/provider or platform change | Are behaviour, permissions, oversight and human effort still acceptable on the supported combination? | [EX08](../examples/ex08.md); LGIRS-4.5 |
| Coding and maintenance agent | Are writes bounded, changes reviewable, tests genuine and recovery conflict-aware? | Adapt EX05 and EX08 to branches, deployment and production effects |
| Multi-agent coordination | Do task intent, limits, evidence and actual states survive each handover? | Adapt EX02 and EX04; preserve identities and authority across actors |

These routes are starting points, not exhaustive mappings or findings of legal applicability.

## Role-specific use

**Product and service leaders:** define the purpose, acceptable experience and actual authority. Ask whether agentic delegation is useful for this task at all, including a supported non-agent route.

**Designers and researchers:** translate principles into usable task contracts, status, explanations, controls and questions for affected people. Connect Cognitive UX and POURS without assigning feelings to participants.

**Engineers and architects:** implement effective permission boundaries, truthful operation state, source context, versioned contracts and evaluated recovery. Keep durable authority and business invariants outside prompt-only assumptions.

**QA, accessibility and security specialists:** combine the relevant standards, manual/AT checks, adversarial and partial-effect scenarios, and actual experience evidence. A single scanner or simulated user cannot replace the full evidence plan.

**Governance and policy professionals:** distinguish candidate guidance from actual duties, proposed commitments from approved ones, and generated records from authenticated decisions. Use the local documentation without requiring technical schema editing.

**Procurement teams:** request evidence for the actual supported deployment, intervention limits and changed model/tool versions. Treat a vendor's protocol compatibility statement as an integration claim to examine, not an LGIR+S certificate.
