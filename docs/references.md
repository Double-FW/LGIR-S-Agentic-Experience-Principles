## Authority and currency

WCAG supplies the actual requirements when assessing a declared WCAG target. W3C Understanding pages explain those requirements and are informative. RFC references supply uppercase keyword conventions. MCP/OpenAPI describe specified integration mechanisms; their use does not establish correctness, safety or universal interoperability. OWASP and NIST provide external security/risk context, not LGIR+S certification.

The protocol editions are deliberately pinned examples, not advice that these are the newest releases. Check actual supported editions and changes before implementation. Access dates are not renewed legal or scientific verification dates. No legal framework is inferred solely from a service category or a linked repository title.

Double-FW materials inform the local methods and intended outcomes. A preferred Double-FW method cannot redefine a statute, another publisher’s standard or an applicable contractual obligation. Record differences and unresolved questions rather than silently changing external meanings.

The owner-supplied Agentic Robustness definition is attributed in data/framework.json and the specification. Other detailed wording is editorial synthesis prepared for this project. Examples are fictional; no external case-study effectiveness is implied.

No unpublished glossary, private Double-FW repository, personal Library locator or account credential is required. No full third-party standard is bundled.
